# DART artwork photo sources

All 25 source photographs were supplied by the user in the local Picture/type inventory. They show the user's own DART artwork and commissioned products. These assets replace the museum samples in src/data/artworks.ts. No external museum artwork or generated imagery is used in the inventory.

The images are supplied for this DART site; no public-domain or Creative Commons license is asserted. Character names Akaza and Raiden come from the user-provided filenames. Portrait subjects are not identified. Other artwork titles are descriptive editorial titles based on visual inspection.

## Processing

Each source was visually inspected, auto-oriented from EXIF with Sharp.rotate(), resized proportionally to fit within 1,600 × 1,600 pixels without enlargement, and encoded as WebP at quality 84. No cropping, retouching, color adjustment, or image generation was applied. Source photos remain untouched; their hashes were checked before and after processing. Every output was decoded successfully.
